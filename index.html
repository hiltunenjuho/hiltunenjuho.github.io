01 / MY PATH

A non-traditional path into software

My route into software development has been hands-on rather than conventional. Earlier programming studies and personal projects gave me a foundation, but the turning point came when I started noticing repetitive work, disconnected systems, and frustrating interfaces in real business workflows.

I learned by applying what I knew to real problems, building JavaScript tools, automations, and integrations instead of stopping at exercises. Seeing software make everyday work simpler turned a long-standing interest into a clear career goal.

02 / WHAT DRIVES ME

For me,

software development is a mix of problem-solving, creativity and continuous learning.

Whether I'm developing a web app, exploring a new technology or improving an existing product,

I enjoy the whole process of turning ideas into working solutions.

03 / HOW I WORK

Start with the real problem

I look for the point where a workflow loses time, information, or clarity. Then I learn how the work is actually done and build a solution around the people who use it.

My projects have included tools that bring useful information together, reduce repetitive steps, and make complex workflows easier to navigate. I care about the difference a solution makes, not just the technologies behind it.

04 / WHAT'S NEXT

Building toward what's next

Right now, I’m working toward a full-time role in software development, bringing a practical understanding of business workflows and experience building tools for real needs.

I’m continuing to deepen my skills, while exploring how AI can make useful systems work even better.

My focus stays the same: understand the problem, build thoughtfully, and keep learning.
